### katabame-chan v1.3 Detailed Specification


#### Physique
* Height: Around 160cm
* Bust: B to C cup
* Fair skin (Requirement)
* Long fingers


#### Clothing
* Usually wears an oversized T-shirt
    * Sleeves are slightly long
* Long skirt


#### Wings
* Right wing: The base is torn off
    * katabane-chan
* Shape: Assumed to be bird-like (Recommendation)
    * It can also be a butterfly, a bat, or anything else
* Minimum (normal) size: Palm size, about 15-16cm
* Maximum size: Height + 10cm (about 170cm)


#### Face
* Expressions tend not to show emotions
* Always looks sleepy
* Does not wear glasses (Recommendation)


#### Hair

* Bangs: Round bangs
* Hair ornament: Shaped like an oxalis (flower|leaf)
* Ahoge (cowlick): None (Requirement)
